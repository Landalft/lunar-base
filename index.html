    10The small print

    What is real here

    Public NASA data only. No SLB data. Nothing private.

    • Real: every map, photo and terrain surface. NASA public data.
    • Ours: the 3D model under the surface, calculated from NASA gravity.
    • Invented: the ground under each square, and the ten-year story.
    Sources and method
    • Real, from NASA: the Aristarchus photo (LROC camera), terrain and slope (LOLA laser; south pole grid by Barker et al. 2023, PGDA product 90), gravity (GRAIL) and south-pole hydrogen (Lunar Prospector). The steep squares at the south pole are real slopes.
    • Real physics, simple rules: lunar gravity is 1.62 m/s², a sixth of Earth's. Weight and ground pressure come from your design with standard formulas and round figures.
    • Our own model: the 3D density blocks, calculated for this capstone from GRAIL gravity. One possible answer, not the only one.
    • The instruments: real tools, used on Earth and on past Moon and Mars missions. Here each one is simplified to a single question.
    • Invented: the ground under every square, what each map shows, and the ten-year timeline. They show what can go wrong. They are not predictions for these places.
    • Rigged, on purpose: your first two builds always fail. Wherever you tap, we put the hazard there. With no ground data, nobody could tell you otherwise. The "100 builders" numbers are not rigged.
    An explainer, not a product. Made for the Imperial EMBA capstone "SLB Lunar Intelligence". It is a capstone concept, not an SLB offering, and not an engineering tool.
